Timberstead

Build a town. Weather winter.

Your settlers have reached the frontier. Build them a town, one good choice at a time.

Coming soon to the App Store

Winter is the test

Homes burn wood. Bushes stop giving food. Cold and hunger make people sick, and the herbalist's medicine brings them back. Prepare shows what the winter needs, in time to do something about it.

A town that grows

Start with a camp and a fire. Add a sawmill, a farmhouse and its fields, a barn, a grist mill, a bakery, a schoolhouse, an inn and a market. Turn cabins into frame, brick and fine houses.

Raids and walls

Bandits come at most once a year, with a few days' warning. Draw a palisade, raise watchtowers and train soldiers at the barracks. They take their posts and defend the town by themselves.

Every villager counts

Every settler has a name and a job. Watch them chop, carry, build and farm, and follow anyone through their day.

Play your way

Gentle, Frontier, Hard Winter or Hardcore. Pause, play, or run at double speed whenever you like.

Free to play

The whole game is free, every mode included. One purchase removes the ads for good. No account needed.
