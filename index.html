The town

A year on the frontier

Ten views of the settlement, from the first cabins to the winter wall. Swipe through, or use the arrows.

1 / 10

01 — The clearing

Build and grow

A camp and a fire, then a town. Raise log cabins and frame houses, a chapel and a settlement hall, a sawmill and a farm with its fields. Pick a building, drag it into the clearing, and set it down.

02 — The long cold

Survive the winter

Winter is the test. Homes burn wood. The wild goes quiet, and cold and hunger make people sick. The herbalist's medicine brings them back. Prepare shows the food, beds, medicine, and wood the town needs, in time to do something about it.

03 — Names

Families and villagers

Every settler has a name, a job, and needs of their own. Follow anyone through the day: health, tiredness, and the work in their hands. As the years go on, families form. Weddings, husbands, children. The cabins fill.

04 — The wall

Defend the town

Bandits come at most once a year, and the town gets a few days' warning. Draw a palisade, raise watchtowers, and train soldiers at the barracks. They take their posts and hold the wall.

05 — The year

Four seasons, one town

Spring opens the ground. Summer is for building. Autumn fills the sheds. Winter asks for everything you stored. The same paths, the same hall, a different kind of work.

06 — Ore and iron

Mine and smith

Sink a mine at the edge of the clearing and raise a blacksmith by the road. Ore comes up out of the hill. The smith works it into the tools and ironwork a growing town needs.

How a year feels

Play it your way

  • Gentle
  • Frontier
  • Hard Winter
  • Hardcore

On Hardcore, a town is deleted for good when everyone in it dies. The other modes always keep a few people alive. Pause, play, or run at double speed whenever you like.
