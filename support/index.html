Help with Timberstead for iPhone

Something wrong, a question, or an idea? Email us and we'll get back to you. It helps to include your iPhone model, the iOS version, and what you were doing when the problem happened. A screenshot is great too.

Email: persueapps@gmail.com

Common questions

Where are my towns saved?

On your iPhone, and in its normal backups (iCloud Backup or your computer). There's no account. If you move to a new iPhone by restoring a backup, your towns come with you. Deleting the app deletes its towns.

How do I remove the ads?

Open Settings in the game (the gear button) and tap Remove Ads. It's a one-time purchase through the App Store, and it removes every ad for good.

I bought Remove Ads but I'm seeing ads on a new device

Open Settings in the game and tap Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple account you bought it with. If that doesn't work, email us.

How do I get a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple. You can ask Apple for a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

How do I change my ad privacy choices?

Tracking permission is in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. In Europe and the UK, Timberstead's Settings also has Ad Privacy Choices. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Can I play offline?

Yes. The whole game plays without a connection. Ads only appear when you're online.

What happens on Hardcore if everyone dies?

On Hardcore, a town is deleted for good when everyone in it dies. The other modes always keep a few people alive.

My people are idle or my town ran short in winter

Tap People to see who is idle and give them a job. Before winter, open Prepare from the winter card to see how much wood, food and medicine the town needs.
