Privacy Policy

Timberstead for iPhone · Effective November 17, 2026

Timberstead is a single-player town-building game made by Persue Apps ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the game uses, who receives it, and the choices you have. In short: your towns stay on your device, we don't run any servers or collect your data ourselves, and the ads in the free game come from Google AdMob.

1. What stays on your device

Your towns, settings and progress are saved on your iPhone and in its normal device backups (iCloud or your computer, if you use them). We never receive them. Timberstead has no account and no sign-in. Deleting the app deletes your towns, unless they are in a backup you keep.

2. Information we collect

We, Persue Apps, do not collect, store or sell any personal information from the game. We have no analytics in Timberstead and no server that the game talks to. If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to write, and we use it only to answer you.

3. Ads (Google AdMob)

Timberstead is free and shows full-screen ads from time to time while you play, unless you buy Remove Ads. These ads are provided by Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. To choose, show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google's software in the app may collect and process:

DataWhat it is
Device identifiersYour device's advertising identifier (IDFA), only if you allow tracking; otherwise an app-specific identifier that can't follow you to other companies' apps.
Approximate locationA rough location worked out from your IP address. Timberstead never asks for or uses your precise location.
Device and app detailsDevice model, operating system version, language and similar technical details.
Ad interactionsWhich ads were shown and whether they were tapped.
DiagnosticsCrash and performance data about the ad software.

This information goes to Google, not to us. Google's use of it is covered by the Google Privacy Policy; see also How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Your choices about ads

  • Tracking permission. The first time ads are set up, iOS asks whether Timberstead may track you across other companies' apps and websites. If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", ads are still shown but are not personalized using your advertising identifier. You can change this any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • European Economic Area, United Kingdom and Switzerland. Before any ad is shown, Google's consent form asks for your choices about personalized ads. You can change them later from Timberstead's Settings → Ad Privacy Choices.
  • Remove Ads. Buying Remove Ads turns ads off for good on your Apple account, and the ad software then shows nothing. Restore Purchases in Settings brings it back on a new device.
  • Apple's personalized ads setting in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Apple Advertising applies to Apple's own ads, not to ads in Timberstead.

4. Purchases

Remove Ads is sold through Apple's App Store. Apple handles the payment; we never see your payment details, name or Apple ID. The game only learns from Apple whether Remove Ads is owned on your account. Apple's handling of your information is described in Apple's Privacy Policy.

5. Crash reports

If you have chosen in iOS to share analytics with app developers, Apple may send us anonymous crash reports and usage statistics. These do not identify you, and we use them only to fix problems in the game.

6. Children

Timberstead is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The game itself collects none. If you believe a child has sent us personal information by email, contact us and we will delete it.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in Europe or the CCPA in California), you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal information, or to object to its use. Because we don't hold any personal information from the game, there is usually nothing for us to access or delete. For data Google processes for ads, use the choices above or Google's own tools at myaccount.google.com. We do not sell personal information. You can always contact us with a question or request.

8. Security and retention

Your towns are protected by your device's own security. We keep emails you send us only as long as needed to help you.

9. Changes to this policy

If the game starts using information in a new way, we will update this page and change the date at the top before that version of the game is released.
